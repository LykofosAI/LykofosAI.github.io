In the exciting world of bootstrapped AI, automation is our superpower. It allows lean teams to achieve incredible scale, handle repetitive tasks, and deliver value around the clock without constant manual intervention. We build these elegant, intricate systems, watch them hum along, and revel in the efficiency gains. But here's a truth that often gets whispered rather than shouted: your automation *will* break. It’s not a matter of if, but when. And for bootstrapped founders, a broken automation pipeline isn't just an inconvenience; it can mean lost revenue, frustrated customers, and a significant drain on precious time and resources. This eleventh installment in our series is dedicated to facing this reality head-on and equipping you with the strategies to not just survive, but thrive, when your beautifully crafted systems hit a snag.
