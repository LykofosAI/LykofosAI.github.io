South Korea, a land often celebrated for its dynamic cities, ancient traditions, and vibrant pop culture, holds a secret many international travelers overlook: it's an absolute dream for road trips. Beyond the bustling metropolises, a network of well-maintained roads winds through stunning mountain ranges, along dramatic coastlines, and past charming rural villages. Renting a car offers an unparalleled freedom to explore the country at your own pace, uncovering hidden gems and experiencing the authentic heart of Korea. If you're ready to ditch the trains and buses for an adventure on four wheels, buckle up. We're about to explore the best driving routes around Korea that promise unforgettable memories.

## The East Coast Grandeur: Gangwon-do's Coastal Highway

Imagine a drive where emerald mountains plunge into the deep blue East Sea, where fishing villages dot the shoreline, and sunrise is a daily spectacle. This is the magic of Gangwon-do's coastal highway, stretching from cities like Gangneung up to Sokcho, and further south towards Samcheok. This region is a perennial favorite among Koreans for good reason, offering a blend of natural beauty and cultural experiences.

Starting from Gangneung, you can explore the picturesque Gyeongpo Lake and its surrounding beaches before heading north. The road frequently offers panoramic ocean views, allowing spontaneous stops at secluded coves or lively seaside towns. Don't miss Jeongdongjin, famous for its beachside train station and being one of the best places in Korea to watch the sunrise. Further north, Sokcho provides access to Seoraksan National Park, a hiker's paradise, while its fishing port offers some of the freshest seafood you'll ever taste. For those driving south of Gangneung, you'll find charming towns like Donghae and Samcheok, known for their unique caves and serene beaches. This route is particularly stunning in spring when cherry blossoms line the roads, or in autumn when the mountain foliage bursts into fiery colors.

## Island Paradise Loop: Jeju Island's Coastal Road (Haean-ro)

No list of Korean driving routes would be complete without Jeju Island. Often called 'the Hawaii of Korea,' this volcanic island boasts a unique landscape, distinct culture, and a pace of life that feels refreshingly slow compared to the mainland. The most popular and rewarding way to experience Jeju is by driving its circumferential coastal road, often referred to as 'Haean-ro' in various sections.

This roughly 200-kilometer loop offers constant, breathtaking views of the ocean, dramatic volcanic cliffs, and verdant fields. You can circumnavigate the entire island in a day, but that would be a disservice to its charms. Take your time to stop at iconic spots like Seongsan Ilchulbong (Sunrise Peak), the majestic Jeongbang Waterfall (one of the few waterfalls in Asia that falls directly into the ocean), and the numerous black sand beaches. The western coast, particularly around Aewol and Hyeopjae, is famous for its trendy cafes and beautiful sunsets, while the eastern side offers more rugged beauty. Renting a compact car is ideal here, allowing you to easily navigate the island's winding roads and park in smaller towns. Jeju truly allows you to disconnect and immerse yourself in nature's splendor.
