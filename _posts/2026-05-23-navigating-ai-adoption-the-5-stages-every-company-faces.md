The promise of Artificial Intelligence often feels like a shiny new object, a magical solution poised to revolutionize every industry overnight. While the potential is undeniable, the reality of integrating AI into a traditional business isn't a single switch you flip. Instead, it's a dynamic, multi-stage journey, often fraught with both excitement and frustration. Understanding these predictable stages isn't just academic; it’s a crucial roadmap for any company looking to move beyond the hype and truly harness AI's power. Ignoring them can lead to wasted resources, stalled projects, and missed opportunities. Let's explore the five distinct phases every company navigates on its path to AI maturity.
