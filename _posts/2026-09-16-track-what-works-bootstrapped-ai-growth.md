In the whirlwind world of building a bootstrapped AI business, every minute, every dollar, and every line of code counts. You’re often wearing multiple hats – developer, marketer, sales rep, customer support, and visionary. With so much on your plate and limited resources, it’s easy to fall into the trap of doing *a lot* without truly knowing what’s actually moving the needle. You *think* that new marketing strategy is working, or that complex feature you just shipped will be a game-changer. But how do you *know*? How do you separate the impactful activities from the time sinks? This is where a simple, yet profoundly effective, tracking method comes into play, ensuring your precious time and energy are always directed towards what truly works.
